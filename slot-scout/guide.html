Slot Scout

How it works

Slot Scout watches the AIS appointment calendar and books an earlier slot when one opens up between the dates you pick.

Free, and your details stay with you

Slot Scout is free. There's nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay, and no credits to run out of. You use your normal AIS login — the same email and password you already have.

Your login and your appointment details stay in this browser. The only site the extension talks to is ais.usvisa-info.com, the one you're already signed in to.

What you need first

  • An appointment that is already booked. This reschedules an existing appointment; it can't create a first one.
  • An AIS country that uses the standard scheduling system.
  • The tab left open. Closing it stops the watch.

The steps

  1. Sign in. Use your normal AIS email and password. Saving them is optional — leave it off and you sign in yourself each time the site logs you out.
  2. Pick the appointment if the account holds more than one, and confirm the date you currently hold.
  3. Set your window. Only slots between these two dates count. Anything outside is ignored, so you don't get moved to a date that's worse for you.
  4. Rank your locations — up to three — then set how often to check and whether to book automatically.
  5. When a slot appears, it fills the form and either confirms it or asks you first, depending on the auto-book setting below.

Auto-book, and what happens without it

Auto-book is on by default, and that is the setting to keep unless you have a reason not to. Earlier slots are taken within seconds of appearing. If Slot Scout has to wait for a human, the slot is usually gone before the human answers.

With auto-book on: the form is filled, submitted and confirmed in one go. You get a notification telling you it happened.

With auto-book off, here is exactly what happens:

  1. The form is filled in and submitted, so the site's own confirmation dialog is open and ready.
  2. You are asked on two surfaces at once — a desktop notification, which reaches you even when the browser is behind another window, and a dialog on the page itself. Answering either one dismisses the other.
  3. You have three minutes. A countdown runs on the dialog.
  4. Choose Book it and it confirms. Choose Skip this one and it cancels and goes back to watching. Do nothing, and after three minutes it cancels and goes back to watching on its own.

The site does not hold the slot while you decide

Nothing is reserved during those three minutes. The wait is short precisely because a longer one would just mean trying to confirm a slot somebody else already took. If you cannot reliably answer within three minutes, leave auto-book on.

Watching more than one location

You can rank up to three consulates. Slot Scout checks one per cycle, in your order — first cycle the top choice, next cycle the second, and so on.

This matters: it means extra locations cost you nothing. The number of requests per hour is identical whether you watch one location or three, so your risk of being blocked does not go up. The trade-off is coverage — with two locations at a five-minute interval, each one is checked every ten minutes.

The biometrics support centre stays a single choice, shared across every consulate you watch.

Getting blocked

Checking faster does not help

After a certain number of checks the site stops returning any dates at all for a few hours. That's a soft block. If you keep hammering it during one, it escalates to a longer lockout that can cover your whole IP address.

Slot Scout treats an empty calendar as a soft-block signal and backs off automatically — 30 minutes, then 45, then up to 2 hours — instead of retrying at your normal interval. Leave it alone and it recovers on its own.

The default 5-minute interval is a reasonable balance. Below 2 minutes you're trading a real risk of lockout for very little extra coverage.

Settings

Setting What it does
Not before / Not after The date window. A slot outside it is skipped even if it's earlier than what you hold.
Check every Base interval between checks. Random jitter of about 10% is added so repeated checks aren't perfectly regular.
Book automatically On (default): confirms the booking the moment a slot is found. Off: asks you, and releases the slot after three minutes if you don't answer.
Locations, in order Up to three consulates, checked one per cycle in this order. Changing location can mean a much longer trip — check before adding one.
Biometrics date preference Whether to take the earliest or latest available support centre appointment once a consulate slot is found.

If something goes wrong

  • Status stuck on "Backing off" — you're soft blocked. Nothing to fix; it clears in a few hours.
  • "Needs attention" — open the side panel; the last result line says what happened.
  • Signed out over and over — the site does this on its own. If you saved your login it signs back in; if not, you'll need to.
  • Anything else — Reset everything in the side panel clears all saved data and starts over.

Rescheduling limits

AIS caps how many times an appointment can be rescheduled. Going over that cap can cancel your appointment outright — that's the site's rule, not something the extension can work around. If you see the site's limit warning, take it seriously.
