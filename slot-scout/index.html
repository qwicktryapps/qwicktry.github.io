Slot Scout How it works Privacy

Earlier US visa appointments, without the refreshing

Slot Scout is a free Chrome extension that checks the AIS visa calendar for you and books an earlier appointment when one opens up between the dates you pick. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay.

Version 1.0.0 · not yet on the Chrome Web Store

Why this one

Actually free

No sign-up, no access code, no paid version, no credits that run out. Every feature works for everyone, always.

Your details stay put

You use your normal AIS login. Your email, password and appointment details stay in your browser — the only site it talks to is the one you're already signed in to.

Won't get you blocked

When the site stops showing dates, it has blocked you. Slot Scout slows down instead of pushing harder, because pushing turns a few hours into a few days.

How it works

  1. Sign in as usual

    Your normal AIS email and password. Saving them is optional and off by default — leave it off and you sign in yourself when the site logs you out.

  2. Pick your dates

    Only slots between two dates count. Anything outside is ignored, so you never get moved to a date that's worse for you.

  3. Choose up to three places

    It checks one each time, in the order you pick. Extra places are free — it never checks more often whether you watch one consulate or three.

  4. Leave the tab open

    A panel shows what it's doing, when the next check is, and what the last one found.

  5. It books the slot

    Automatically, because slots go within seconds. Turn that off and you get a notification plus three minutes to decide for yourself.

Before you try it

Honest limitations

  • You need an AIS account and an appointment that's already booked. This moves an existing appointment; it can't book your first one.
  • Your country must use the AIS system at ais.usvisa-info.com. Newer visa sites aren't supported.
  • AIS limits how many times you can reschedule, and going over can cancel your appointment. That's the site's rule and no extension can get around it.
  • Checking too often gets you blocked for a few hours. Slot Scout is set to check slowly on purpose.
