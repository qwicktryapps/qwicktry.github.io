Slot Scout How it works Privacy

Earlier visa appointments, without the refreshing

Slot Scout watches the AIS appointment calendar and takes an earlier slot when one opens inside a date range you choose. It is free, it has no server, and nothing you type ever leaves your browser.

Version 1.0.0 · not yet on the Chrome Web Store

Why this one

No server

The only site it contacts is the one you are already signed into. There is no backend, so there is nowhere your data could go.

No credits

No account, no access code, no premium tier. Every feature works for everyone, permanently.

Backs off properly

An empty calendar means the site has throttled you. Slot Scout retreats instead of hammering, because hammering turns hours of lockout into days.

How it works

  1. Sign in as usual

    Saving your password is optional and off by default. Leave it off and you sign in by hand when the site drops your session.

  2. Set your window

    Only slots between two dates count. Anything outside is ignored, so you never get moved to a date that is worse for you.

  3. Rank up to three locations

    One is checked per cycle, in your order. Extra locations cost nothing — the request rate is the same whether you watch one consulate or three.

  4. Leave the tab open

    A status panel shows what it is doing, when the next check is, and what the last one found.

  5. It books the slot

    Automatically by default, because slots vanish in seconds. Turn that off and you get a notification plus three minutes to decide.

Before you try it

Honest limitations

  • You need an appointment that is already booked. This reschedules an existing appointment and cannot create a first one.
  • Your country must use the AIS system at ais.usvisa-info.com. The newer portals are not supported.
  • AIS limits how many times an appointment may be rescheduled, and going over that limit can cancel it. That is the site's rule and no extension can work around it.
  • Checking too often gets you temporarily blocked. Slot Scout defaults to a relaxed interval on purpose.
