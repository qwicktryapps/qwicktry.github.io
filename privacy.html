2 What Information We Collect

AtoZ Shift Helper is designed to collect the absolute minimum. Almost everything stays on your device.

2.1 Stored Locally on Your Device Only

The following data is saved to Chrome's local extension storage on your device. It never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

DataPurposeSent to us?
Your filter preferences (dates, days, time windows, priority windows)Remember your settings between sessionsNever
Your profile label (optional name/note)Displayed on the floating status panelNever
Activity logLocal record of shifts claimed (VET) and time off accepted (VTO) for youNever

2.2 Sent to Amazon's AtoZ Platform

When the extension is running, it communicates directly with Amazon's AtoZ scheduling platform on your behalf — the same platform you use when you log in normally. This is how it reads available opportunities and, with your settings, claims VET / READY shifts or accepts VTO for you. This includes:

DataPurposeSent to us?
Your Amazon Employee IDRead from AtoZ's own page/session and included in the request to AtoZ's API — this is how Amazon's own API works. It is not stored.Never
Available shift dataRead from Amazon's servers so your filters can be applied locallyNever
Note about Amazon's servers The extension communicates with atoz.amazon.work and atoz-apps.amazon.work — Amazon's own systems. This is the same connection your browser makes when you use AtoZ normally. Qwicktry has no access to this communication and receives none of this data. Amazon's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of their platform.

2.3 Our Servers

AtoZ Shift Helper does not communicate with any Qwicktry server. We do not operate a backend, database, or verification service for this extension. No data is ever sent to us.

We store nothing about you Qwicktry holds no database records for extension users. We have no server that receives data from the extension. Everything runs locally on your device.

2.4 This Website

The qwicktry.com website does not use tracking cookies, analytics services (such as Google Analytics), or any third-party tracking scripts. We do not collect information about visitors to our website.

2.5 Support Communications

If you contact us at support@qwicktry.com, we receive and store your email address and message content solely to respond to you. We do not share this with third parties and retain support emails only as long as necessary to resolve your issue.

3 How We Use Your Information

Because the extension stores everything locally and sends nothing to Qwicktry, we have no user data to use. The only information we may hold is:

  • Support emails: Used solely to respond to your inquiry — never shared or used for any other purpose

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose beyond responding to direct support requests.

4 How We Protect Your Information

  • Local storage only: Your preferences are stored in Chrome's local extension storage — inaccessible to any website or third party, never transmitted to us
  • HTTPS for Amazon communication: All communication between the extension and Amazon's AtoZ platform uses HTTPS encryption (TLS)
  • No Qwicktry backend: We have no server receiving your data, so there is no Qwicktry server to compromise
  • Minimal data: The extension only stores what is strictly necessary for it to function

Data stored locally on your device is entirely under your control. You can delete it at any time by clearing the extension's storage or uninstalling the extension.

5 Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies.

Because the extension does not send any data to Qwicktry, there is nothing we could share. The only third-party communication is between the extension and Amazon's own AtoZ platform — the same platform you use directly as an employee.

Legal disclosure We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request. In practice, since we hold no user data from the extension, this would apply only to any support emails you have sent us.

6 Cookies and Tracking

The AtoZ Shift Helper extension does not use cookies.

The qwicktry.com website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any analytics services.

The only local data storage the extension uses is Chrome's chrome.storage.local API — this is browser storage on your own device only, completely inaccessible to any website or third party.

7 Your Rights

We honour these rights for all users regardless of where you live:

Right to Access

You can request a copy of any information we hold about you. Since the extension stores everything locally and sends nothing to us, the only data we may hold is any support email correspondence you have had with us.

Right to Deletion

You can delete all data the extension stores by uninstalling the extension or clearing Chrome's extension storage. For any support email correspondence, contact us and we will delete it.

Right to Correction

If any information we hold is inaccurate, you can request that we correct it.

How to Exercise Your Rights

Email support@qwicktry.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" and describe your request. We will respond within 30 days.

Canadian Residents (PIPEDA)

You have rights under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca if you have concerns.

European Residents (GDPR)

You have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California Residents (CCPA)

You have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We do not sell personal information. Contact support@qwicktry.com to exercise your rights.

8 Data Retention

  • Extension data (local): Stored on your device only — retained until you clear it or uninstall the extension. Entirely under your control.
  • Support emails: Retained for up to 2 years or until you request deletion

Qwicktry holds no server-side records for extension users.

Our extension is intended for use by Amazon associates who are adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you are under 18, please do not use our extension or provide any information to us.
