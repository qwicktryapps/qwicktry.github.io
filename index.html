Up and running in minutes

Four steps. That's it.

The same four steps as the video — tap any step to watch it.

1

Install it

Add AtoZ Shift Helper to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store, then pin it so it's one click away. About 30 seconds.

2

Open it on AtoZ

Go to your Find Shifts page for VET and READY shifts, or your VTO page for time off. Click the extension's icon and pick the matching mode.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What does it actually do? +
It watches Amazon AtoZ for available opportunities and automatically claims the ones that match your filters. In VET / READY mode it picks up extra shifts the moment they post; in VTO mode it accepts Voluntary Time Off as soon as it opens. You set the filters once — it does the grabbing for you, far faster than refreshing by hand.
What's the difference between VET and VTO? +
VET (Voluntary Extra Time) and READY shifts are extra hours you can pick up on top of your schedule — VET mode claims them for you. VTO (Voluntary Time Off) is the opposite — a chance to take time off — and VTO mode accepts it for you. Switch between them anytime in the popup.
How does it grab shifts so fast? +
While it's running, it checks AtoZ on a tight loop — as often as twice a second — and claims any matching opportunity the instant it appears. That's the difference between getting the shift and watching someone else take it.
What do I need to get started? +
Just Google Chrome and an active Amazon AtoZ account. Install the extension, set your filters, and you're ready.
Is my account information safe? +
Yes. Everything runs locally in your own browser. Your filters are stored on your device only — they're never sent to us. The extension talks only to AtoZ's own scheduling pages to read and claim shifts on your behalf, and it never touches your activity on any other site.
Which browser does it work on? +
Google Chrome. It's available on the Chrome Web Store and works on recent versions of Chrome.
